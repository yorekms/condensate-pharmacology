Companion Paper #1 • Preprint Draft (Passphrase-Protected)

Phase-Partitioned Allosteric Pharmacology: Brush-Insulated Signaling Complexes & Phase-Gated PDE4D

Matthew S. Yorek, M.S. & Johannes W. Hell, Ph.D.* (Invited Co-Author, UC Davis)

Integrates 5.45 µs of CALVADOS-2/3 GPU molecular dynamics across human, cold-water fish, and avian orthologs with a 1,093-drug FDA screen and PDB: 3G4G docking to explain nanometer-scale cAMP containment and resolve six classic pharmacological paradoxes.

  • 8/8 AKAPs & ADCY5 form cold-immune polymer brushes vs. PKA/thermally gated PDE4D sinks
  • Coupled Models I–IV (Cromolyn cliff, Zatolmilast 64× S_nano, Lumacaftor hormesis, spine PDE)
  • Includes interactive 3D PDB: 3G4G pocket viewer & phase-binding kinetics cockpit

Companion Paper #2 • Strategy & Design (Passphrase-Protected)

Phase-Aware Pharmacological Strategy, Proteome & Channelome IDR Modulatability & Generative Drug Design

Matthew S. Yorek, M.S. (University of Virginia Anesthesiology)

Scales condensate pharmacology across 61 flagship clinical drugs, ClinVar-pathogenic condensate genetics, 12 experimental human PDBs + 4-target AutoDock Vina validation, proteome & 62-channel cAMP/ATP/phospho IDR targetability maps, CMGC kinome GPU MD, and 1,097 real human clinical IV drugs.

  • 122-target proteome + 62-channel cAMP/ATP/phospho AFDB v6 audit (direct ATP 0/9 vs. cAMP/phospho 36/53, p = 1.54×10⁻⁴)

  • Includes interactive 12-PDB multi-drug 3D exit-vector viewer & gas-anesthetic Model V
