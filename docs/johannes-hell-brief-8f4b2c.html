  • Why AKAP79/150 & ADCY5 stay fluid while PDE4D condenses: 8/8 organellar AKAPs (Q = −23 to −30, 86–95% Lys) & ADCY5 stay soluble even at 5 °C (1.35×), whereas PDE4D condenses upon dimerization + PKA S190/S197 (4.3× → 30.5×)—the exact QRRESF residue ablated by ClinVar pathogenic Acrodysostosis-2 p.Ser190Ala—while CaV1.2 S1700/S1928 shifts in the opposite direction toward C-tail extension (Patriarchi et al. 2016).
  • Evolutionary thermal calibration: Cold-water fish PDE4D (5 °C, 8.5× softer than human 374× collapse) vs. human fever melt (42 °C, 2.0×) vs. chicken (41 °C, 12.8×) and causal avian Tyr swap (S6Y/S18Y/S145Y) rescuing human PDE4D at 42 °C.
